# Adaptive revision design

## Evidence and scope

The source archive targets Integrated Care A5 and the Pharmacology final: A5 is 29 October 2026, 10:00–12:00 NZDT, 40%, with MCQ, true/false and short answers; Max supplied the Pharmacology date and weight as “50% Nov 2”.

Pharmacology follows the ten study-guide chapters and section objectives; Integrated Care follows the six teaching weeks, with assessment, equity, cultural safety and nursing action integrated into cases.

Source references retain original filenames, PDF page or slide numbers, and short evidence excerpts; raw capture pages and session metadata remain outside the deployed site.

## Learning loop

Every recommendation opens an exam-style question, with the drug pathway or person-centred map showing the knowledge to reconstruct; missing nodes hold the questions and fill in as the learner checks the pieces.

The first decision is “I know it” or “I don’t know it”, with optional written recall; after revealing the rubric, Easy, Good, Hard or “I missed it” update FSRS, while an unknown answer opens its prerequisite questions before returning to the original task.

Seeing an answer and succeeding immediately after help are learning events, not independent evidence of exam readiness; the next spaced, unassisted answer supplies that evidence.

## Allocation and progress

FSRS controls recall intervals; a separate pure prioritiser combines assessment date and weight, due/forgotten items, gaps, and prerequisite connections, with a rolling guard against neglecting either active course.

Home has one recommended activity and its reason; the separate curriculum view shows assessed understanding, untested questions, reviews due, and source gaps without offering a course-selection study flow or claiming a predicted exam score.

## Interruption and verification

Drafts, the current question, prerequisite stack, ratings and reviews persist in IndexedDB; a rating and the resulting session transition commit together, so leaving at any point retains useful progress.

Verification must cover the unknown → visual prerequisites → original question flow, corrected self-assessment, due reviews, fairness and post-exam allocation, content graph/source integrity, storage migration and failure, draft restoration, offline study, and narrow screens with keyboard navigation.

## Written exam practice

Practice-method prompts are authored learning design, identified separately from course-derived medical teaching. Identify asks for named facts; compare asks for matched similarities and differences; explain asks for causal links. Allocate available working time in proportion to marks in a hypothetical paper, keep a final-check reserve, move past a stalled question, then return and check instructions, omitted subparts and units. For example, 60 working minutes across 60 marks gives eight minutes to an eight-mark question.
